# Scientific Background

## The Biology of Aging

Aging is characterized by the progressive decline of physiological function over time. The hallmarks of aging include:

**Genomic Instability:** Accumulation of DNA damage and mutations.

**Telomere Attrition:** Shortening of protective chromosome ends.

**Epigenetic Alterations:** Changes in gene expression patterns.

**Loss of Proteostasis:** Accumulation of misfolded proteins.

**Mitochondrial Dysfunction:** Decline in cellular energy production.

**Cellular Senescence:** Accumulation of non-dividing, dysfunctional cells.

**Stem Cell Exhaustion:** Decline in tissue regeneration capacity.

**Altered Intercellular Communication:** Changes in cell signaling and inflammation.

## Key Concepts in Longevity Research

**Healthspan:** The period of life free from major disease and disability.

**Biological Age:** A measure of physiological function compared to chronological age.

**Senolytics:** Drugs that selectively eliminate senescent cells.

**Epigenetic Clock:** A model that predicts biological age from DNA methylation patterns.

## Finding 2: Senolytic Drugs in Clinical Trials

Senolytic drugs that selectively eliminate senescent cells are being tested in clinical trials.

**Key Senolytic Agents:**
- **Dasatinib + Quercetin:** Combination therapy targeting multiple senescent cell types.
- **Fisetin:** Natural compound with senolytic properties.
- **Navitoclax (ABT-263):** BCL-2 inhibitor with senolytic effects.
- **UBX0101:** Novel senolytic agent in clinical trials.

**Clinical Applications:**
- **Osteoarthritis:** Senolytics for joint health and pain reduction.
- **Pulmonary fibrosis:** Senolytics for lung function improvement.
- **Atherosclerosis:** Senolytics for vascular health.
- **Alzheimer's disease:** Senolytics for cognitive health.

**Evidence Status:** Clinical trials; peer-reviewed publications

## Finding 3: Epigenetic Clocks — Measuring Biological Age

AI-driven epigenetic clocks enable accurate measurement of biological age.

**Key Epigenetic Clocks:**
- **Horvath Clock:** The original epigenetic clock based on DNA methylation.
- **GrimAge:** Second-generation clock predicting mortality and health outcomes.
- **DNAm PhenoAge:** Clock predicting physiological age and disease risk.

**Applications:**
- **Health assessment:** Biological age measurement for individuals.
- **Intervention tracking:** Monitoring the efficacy of longevity interventions.
- **Drug discovery:** Identifying compounds that slow or reverse biological aging.

**Evidence Status:** Peer-reviewed publications; clinical validation
