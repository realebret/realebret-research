# Key Findings

## Finding 1: Small Modular Reactors (SMRs)

SMRs are factory-built reactors with simplified designs and improved safety.

**Key SMR Designs:**
- **Light-water SMRs:** Based on proven LWR technology, smaller scale.
- **Advanced SMRs:** Innovative designs with passive safety features.
- **Microreactors:** Extremely small reactors for remote applications.

**Advantages:**
- **Factory construction:** Lower cost, improved quality control.
- **Simplified safety:** Passive safety systems, reduced accident risk.
- **Flexible deployment:** Scalable to meet demand, suitable for remote locations.

**Applications:**
- **Grid-scale power:** Replacing coal and gas plants.
- **Industrial heat:** Process heat for manufacturing.
- **Remote power:** Mining, military bases, island communities.

**Evidence Status:** Regulatory approvals; commercial deployment

---

## Finding 2: Generation IV Reactors

Generation IV reactors offer advanced designs with improved performance.

**Key Gen IV Designs:**
- **Sodium-cooled fast reactor (SFR):** Liquid sodium coolant, fast neutron spectrum.
- **Gas-cooled fast reactor (GFR):** Helium coolant, high-temperature operation.
- **Lead-cooled fast reactor (LFR):** Liquid lead or lead-bismuth coolant.
- **Molten salt reactor (MSR):** Liquid fuel in molten salt coolant.
- **Supercritical water-cooled reactor (SCWR):** High-temperature water coolant.
- **Very high-temperature reactor (VHTR):** Graphite-moderated, helium-cooled.

**Advantages:**
- **Improved safety:** Passive safety, inherent stability.
- **Higher efficiency:** High-temperature operation for electricity and hydrogen.
- **Waste reduction:** Recycling and burning of nuclear waste.

**Evidence Status:** Research and development; demonstration projects

---

## Finding 3: Molten Salt Reactors

Molten salt reactors offer inherent safety and fuel flexibility.

**Key MSR Concepts:**
- **Liquid fuel:** Fuel dissolved in molten salt coolant.
- **Passive safety:** Freeze plugs prevent criticality accidents.
- **Breeding:** Thorium fuel cycle for sustainable energy.

**Advantages:**
- **Inherent safety:** No high-pressure water, no meltdown risk.
- **Fuel flexibility:** Thorium, uranium, or plutonium fuel.
- **Waste reduction:** Transmutation of long-lived actinides.

**Evidence Status:** Research and development; demonstration projects

---

## Finding 4: Fast Reactors

Fast reactors enable fuel recycling and waste reduction.

**Key Fast Reactor Concepts:**
- **Breeder reactors:** Produce more fuel than they consume.
- **Burner reactors:** Consume nuclear waste and reduce stockpiles.
- **Advanced fuel cycles:** Recycling and transmutation of actinides.

**Advantages:**
- **Fuel efficiency:** High utilization of nuclear fuel.
- **Waste reduction:** Reduced volume and toxicity of nuclear waste.
- **Proliferation resistance:** Improved safeguards for nuclear materials.

**Evidence Status:** Research and development; demonstration projects
